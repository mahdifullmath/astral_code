Who we are

Brud Gaming is a home for people who love games — whether you're chasing leaderboards, discovering hidden indie gems, or just looking for your next weekend binge. We built this site because finding good games shouldn't mean wading through noise.

What you'll find here

  • Curated game pages with cover art, genre, and release info
  • Player reviews and ratings from the community
  • Score tracking so you can see how you stack up
  • No paywalls, no dark patterns — just games

Our approach

We keep things simple and fast. No autoplay videos, no pop-ups, no tracking you across the internet. If a feature doesn't make the experience better, we don't ship it.
